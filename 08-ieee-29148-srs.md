## 1. Scope

### 1.1 System Identification

- **System Name:** Urban Shopper Platform
- **System Type:** Digital marketplace connecting customers with independent shoppers for on-demand purchasing and delivery
- **Version:** 1.0 (MVP + V1 scope as defined in Release Scope)
- **Language:** English (primary specification), Swahili (user-facing language support)

### 1.2 System Purpose

The Urban Shopper Platform is a GPS-enabled mobile application platform that connects customers with verified independent shoppers. Customers submit shopping requests for items from any market or store. The platform automatically assigns the nearest available shopper using a weighted Assignment Score and a 30-second acceptance cascade. The assigned shopper purchases the requested items and delivers them to the customer. The platform handles payment processing via mobile money (M-Pesa, Mixx), dispute resolution, ratings, and all operational aspects.

### 1.3 System Scope

The system SHALL provide:

**In Scope (MVP + V1):**
- Customer mobile application (Android MVP, iOS V1)
- Shopper mobile application (Android MVP, iOS V1)
- GPS-based assignment engine with multi-factor scoring
- Mobile money payment processing (M-Pesa, Mixx, Airtel Money)
- Order lifecycle management with state machine enforcement
- Delivery tracking with dynamic ETA
- Two-way rating system with fraud detection
- Unified dispute resolution framework
- Admin dashboard for operations management
- Multi-language support (Swahili, English)
- Scheduled orders
- Promotions engine
- Escrow integration (V1)

**Out of Scope (Future Versions):**
- Vendor inventory integration (V2)
- Multi-shop orders (V2)
- Corporate/business accounts (V2)
- Batch delivery (V2)
- AI/ML features (V2+)
- International expansion (24+ months)
- Fintech products (shopper loans, insurance) — V2+ via partner model (M-008, M-009)
- In-platform customer wallet — deferred to V1 per F-009

### 1.4 Product Perspective

This is a new greenfield system. It depends on the following external systems:
- Mobile money provider APIs (M-Pesa, Mixx, Airtel Money)
- SMS gateway provider (Africastalking or Twilio)
- Push notification service (Firebase Cloud Messaging)
- Maps/geolocation API (Google Maps Platform / OpenStreetMap)
- Cloud infrastructure provider
- National ID verification (NIDA — as available)

### 1.5 User Characteristics

| Role | Description | Tech Profile | Primary Language |
|------|-------------|-------------|-----------------|
| **Customer** | End user who submits shopping requests and receives deliveries | Moderate — smartphone user, M-Pesa literate | Swahili / English |
| **Shopper** | Independent contractor who shops and delivers items | Moderate — basic Android proficiency | Swahili (primary) |
| **Administrator** | Platform staff managing operations | High — experienced with web dashboards | English |
| **Support Agent** | Platform staff handling disputes | Moderate-High | English / Swahili |

#### 5.1.1 Customer Account Management

**FR-CUST-001: Customer Registration**
The system SHALL allow a prospective customer to register by providing a valid Tanzanian mobile phone number. The system SHALL send an SMS OTP to the provided number. The account SHALL be created only after the customer enters the correct OTP. Registration SHALL require: full name, phone number, and language preference.
*Priority: Critical | BR: A-001, A-003 | UC: UC-001*
*AC: Verify that registration without OTP verification is rejected. Verify that duplicate phone numbers are detected and rejected.*

**FR-CUST-002: Minimum Age Enforcement**
The system SHALL reject registration if the customer indicates they are under 18 years of age.
*Priority: High | BR: A-002 | UC: UC-001*
*AC: Verify that an under-18 selection during registration results in a rejection message.*

**FR-CUST-003: Communication Preferences**
The system SHALL capture and store the customer's preferred language (Swahili or English) and notification channel (push, SMS, in-app) during registration. The customer SHALL be able to change these preferences at any time from their profile.
*Priority: High | BR: A-007 | UC: UC-001*
*AC: Verify that emergency notifications (payment failures, security) are always sent via SMS regardless of preference.*

**FR-CUST-004: Account Deactivation**
The system SHALL allow a customer to deactivate their account at any time. The system SHALL block deactivation if the customer has any pending orders or unresolved disputes. Deactivation SHALL take effect immediately upon confirmation.
*Priority: High | BR: A-004 | UC: UC-001*
*AC: Verify that deactivation is blocked when orders are in active status. Verify that deactivation with no pending orders completes immediately.*

**FR-CUST-005: Account Suspension**
The system SHALL support platform-initiated account suspension for: fraudulent activity, multiple payment failures, abusive behaviour, or violation of terms of service. Suspended customers SHALL be notified with the reason and appeal rights.
*Priority: Critical | BR: A-005 | UC: UC-001*
*AC: Verify that a flagged account cannot place orders. Verify that the customer receives notification with appeal instructions.*

**FR-CUST-006: Customer Trust Score Calculation**
The system SHALL calculate and maintain a Customer Trust Score based on: successful payment history (35%), completed orders (25%), order cancellation rate (15%), account age (10%), shopper ratings received (10%), and dispute history (5%). The score SHALL be recalculated after each order completion.
*Priority: High | BR: F-012 | UC: UC-001*
*AC: Verify that a new customer with no order history has a base score. Verify that a customer with multiple successful payments has a higher score than one with payment failures.*

**FR-CUST-007: Dormant Account Cleanup**
The system SHALL flag customer accounts with no login or order activity for 12 consecutive months as dormant. The system SHALL not archive accounts with a positive wallet balance until the balance is zero.
*Priority: Low | BR: A-008 | UC: UC-001*
*AC: Verify that an account with 12 months of inactivity is flagged. Verify that accounts with balance are not archived.*

**FR-CUST-008: Order History Access**
The system SHALL display the customer's complete order history for the past 12 months in the app. Orders older than 12 months SHALL be available on request within 7 days.
*Priority: Medium | BR: A-010 | UC: UC-001*
*AC: Verify that the last 12 months of orders are displayed. Verify that a request for older orders generates a response within 7 days.*

#### 5.1.2 Shopper Account Management

**FR-SHOP-001: Shopper Registration**
The system SHALL allow a prospective shopper to register by providing: full name, phone number (OTP-verified), government-issued ID (photo upload), live selfie for facial matching, transport type and documents (if motorised), mobile money account, emergency contact, and acceptance of Shopper Terms & Code of Conduct.
*Priority: Critical | BR: B-001, B-002 | UC: UC-007*
*AC: Verify that registration requires all mandatory fields. Verify that OTP verification is enforced. Verify that motorised transport requires valid documents.*

**FR-SHOP-002: Identity Verification**
The system SHALL perform identity verification through: (a) photo of government-issued ID, (b) facial recognition matching the ID photo with a live selfie, (c) manual admin review as fallback. The system SHALL support periodic re-verification triggered by: > 90 days inactivity, suspicious activity, account changes, or 12-month routine checks.
*Priority: Critical | BR: B-002 | UC: UC-007*
*AC: Verify that a clear ID photo + selfie match results in automated pass. Verify that a poor-quality photo routes to manual review. Verify that re-verification is triggered after 90 days of inactivity.*

**FR-SHOP-003: Background Verification**
The system SHALL implement Tanzania-appropriate verification: mandatory NIDA ID check (where available), Police Clearance Certificate submission, mobile money account name-matching. Applicants with verified convictions for violent crimes, theft, or fraud SHALL be automatically disqualified.
*Priority: Critical | BR: B-003 | UC: UC-007*
*AC: Verify that an applicant with a flagged PCC record is rejected. Verify that provisional approval with 60-day PCC deadline is supported. Verify that high-value order shoppers require full clearance.*

**FR-SHOP-004: Onboarding Training**
The system SHALL require all approved shoppers to complete a mandatory onboarding programme covering: app usage, order acceptance, shopping best practices, substitution handling, customer communication, product quality assessment, fraud prevention, safety, emergency procedures, and privacy/data protection. The shopper SHALL pass a knowledge assessment to activate their account.
*Priority: High | BR: B-004 | UC: UC-007*
*AC: Verify that a shopper with incomplete training cannot go Online. Verify that training modules can be retaken if failed.*

**FR-SHOP-005: Shopper Availability Status**
The system SHALL allow a shopper to set their status to Active (Online) or Inactive (Offline). The system SHALL consider a shopper available only when: (a) status is Active, (b) the app is connected with a regular heartbeat signal, (c) GPS data is being received. The system SHALL automatically set a shopper to Inactive if the app heartbeat is lost for a configured timeout (recommended: 2 minutes).
*Priority: Critical | BR: B-005 | UC: UC-008*
*AC: Verify that an Online shopper with GPS + heartbeat receives offers. Verify that an Online shopper without heartbeat for 2+ minutes is auto-set to Inactive. Verify that an offline shopper does NOT receive offers.*

**FR-SHOP-006: Acceptance Rate Tracking**
The system SHALL track each shopper's acceptance rate as (Accepted Orders ÷ Offered Orders) × 100 over a rolling 30-day period. The system SHALL exclude from calculation: platform outages, confirmed GPS/network failures, orders withdrawn before the shopper could respond, and offers during known low-connectivity areas.
*Priority: Critical | BR: B-011 | UC: UC-008*
*AC: Verify that acceptance rate is correctly calculated over 30 days. Verify that excluded events do not affect the rate.*

**FR-SHOP-007: Shopper Suspension**
The system SHALL support progressive suspension for: acceptance rate below 40%, completion rate below 90%, customer rating below 4.0 (after 20+ ratings), substantiated fraud/theft, abusive behaviour, or document failure. Penalties SHALL follow: warning → coaching/retraining → temporary suspension → final review. Permanent removal SHALL be reserved for repeated serious misconduct or fraud.
*Priority: High | BR: B-007 | UC: UC-007*
*AC: Verify that a shopper below threshold receives a warning before suspension. Verify that permanent removal requires repeated serious violations.*

**FR-SHOP-008: Shopper Performance Tiers**
The system SHALL classify shoppers into performance tiers based on: order volume, acceptance rate, completion rate, rating, and on-time delivery percentage. Tiers SHALL influence: (a) assignment priority in the Assignment Score, (b) access to high-value orders, (c) early access to features, (d) recognition badges. Tiers SHALL NOT directly change earnings.
*Priority: Medium | BR: B-008 | UC: UC-007*
*AC: Verify that tier is correctly calculated. Verify that tier affects assignment priority but not payout calculation.*

**FR-SHOP-009: Shopper Wallet**
The system SHALL maintain a shopper wallet tracking: pending earnings (orders completed but not yet settled), available balance (settled earnings), and lifetime earnings. The system SHALL allow the shopper to withdraw available balance to their mobile money account (minimum 2,000 TZS, maximum 200,000 TZS/day). Settlement SHALL occur within 48 hours of order completion.
*Priority: Critical | BR: F-010, F-006 | UC: UC-013*
*AC: Verify that a completed order's earnings appear as pending. Verify that after 48 hours, pending moves to available. Verify that withdrawal limits are enforced.*

**FR-SHOP-010: Shopper Appeal**
The system SHALL allow a shopper to appeal any suspension, tier demotion, or financial penalty within 7 days. The system SHALL send an acknowledgment within 24 hours and a final decision within 5 business days.
*Priority: Medium | BR: B-012 | UC: UC-007*
*AC: Verify that an appeal submitted within 7 days is accepted. Verify that acknowledgment is sent within 24 hours.*

#### 5.1.3 Assignment Engine

**FR-ASGN-001: GPS Ping Ingestion**
The system SHALL receive GPS coordinates from shopper apps at tiered frequencies based on shopper state: Online/waiting — every 30 seconds, Offered — every 10 seconds, Travelling — every 5 seconds, Shopping — every 15 seconds, Delivering — every 5 seconds, Offline — no GPS.
*Priority: Critical | BR: C-001 | UC: UC-008*
*AC: Verify that GPS frequency changes when shopper state changes. Verify that offline shoppers transmit no GPS.*

**FR-ASGN-002: Distance Calculation**
The system SHALL calculate distances using a hybrid method: Step 1 — Haversine formula to identify the nearest 20 active shoppers to the market location. Step 2 — Road distance (Maps API) for those 20 shoppers. Road distance SHALL be used for final ranking, delivery fee calculation, and offer radius validation.
*Priority: Critical | BR: C-002 | UC: UC-009*
*AC: Verify that Haversine filters correctly. Verify that road distance is calculated for the top 20. Verify that fallback to Haversine × 1.5 works when Maps API is unavailable.*

**FR-ASGN-003: Assignment Score Calculation**
The system SHALL calculate an Assignment Score for each eligible shopper before each offer. Score = Distance (35%) + Acceptance Rate (20%) + Completion Rate (15%) + Customer Rating (10%) + Current Workload (10%) + Recent Activity (5%) + Zone Priority (5%). The shopper with the highest score SHALL receive the first offer.
*Priority: Critical | BR: C-011 | UC: UC-008, UC-009*
*AC: Verify that the highest-scoring shopper receives the offer. Verify that new shoppers (first 30 days) have modified weighting (50% distance).*

**FR-ASGN-004: 30-Second Offer Window**
The system SHALL present an order offer to the selected shopper with a 30-second countdown. The system SHALL deliver the offer via: (a) push notification with sound and vibration, (b) in-app alert with countdown timer, (c) SMS fallback if push delivery is not confirmed within 5 seconds. If the shopper does not respond within 30 seconds, the offer SHALL be treated as declined.
*Priority: Critical | BR: C-003, J-003 | UC: UC-009*
*AC: Verify that the countdown is exactly 30 seconds. Verify that SMS fallback triggers at 5 seconds. Verify that timeout is treated as decline.*

**FR-ASGN-005: Offer Cascade**
If a shopper declines or times out, the system SHALL immediately offer the order to the next highest-scoring shopper. The cascade SHALL continue until: (a) a shopper accepts, or (b) 3 minutes have elapsed since the first offer. At the end of a cascade with no acceptance, the system SHALL notify the customer with options: try again, expand radius, increase delivery fee, or cancel.
*Priority: Critical | BR: C-004, D-010 | UC: UC-009*
*AC: Verify that the cascade correctly cycles through shoppers. Verify that cascade stops at 3 minutes. Verify that customer receives actionable options on timeout.*

**FR-ASGN-006: Per-Zone Assignment Configuration**
The system SHALL allow administrators to configure per-zone parameters: Maximum Assignment Radius (road distance), delivery fee base and per-km rates, operating hours, and supply/demand ratio thresholds. These parameters SHALL be applied in real time to the assignment engine.
*Priority: High | BR: C-005, C-006, C-007, B-013 | UC: UC-008*
*AC: Verify that updating a zone parameter takes effect without service restart. Verify that shoppers outside the radius do not receive offers.*

**FR-ASGN-007: Customer Location Privacy**
The system SHALL conceal the customer's exact delivery address until a shopper accepts the order. Before acceptance, the system SHALL display only: the neighbourhood name, approximate distance from market, and estimated earnings.
*Priority: High | BR: C-009 | UC: UC-008, UC-009*
*AC: Verify that the exact address is hidden before acceptance. Verify that the general area is displayed. Verify that the general area is approximate ( ±200m).*

**FR-ASGN-008: GPS Spoofing Detection**
The system SHALL monitor incoming GPS data for: impossible speed (> 200 km/h), sudden coordinate jumps (teleportation), static location while status indicates travel, impossible routes (water, restricted areas), signal quality anomalies, and repeated GPS loss during critical moments. Flagged events SHALL be logged for manual review.
*Priority: High | BR: C-010 | UC: UC-008*
*AC: Verify that a spoofed GPS ping exceeding 200 km/h is flagged. Verify that a single anomalous ping is ignored. Verify that 3+ anomalies in 10 minutes trigger a review.*

#### 5.1.4 Order Management

**FR-ORD-001: Order Creation**
The system SHALL allow a customer to create an order with: at least one item (name, quantity, optional brand, optional unit, optional max price), a market/source location, a delivery location, a delivery time preference (ASAP or scheduled), and a shopping preference (Cheapest Available, Best Quality, or Balanced).
*Priority: Critical | BR: D-001, D-002 | UC: UC-002*
*AC: Verify that order creation requires all mandatory fields. Verify that scheduled orders require a time at least 2 hours in the future.*

**FR-ORD-002: Price Estimation Display**
Before order submission, the system SHALL display a detailed price breakdown: estimated item cost, tiered platform service fee (8/10/12/15% per F-001), delivery fee (zone-configurable per F-002), and total estimated cost.
*Priority: Critical | BR: D-003, F-001, F-002 | UC: UC-002*
*AC: Verify that the price breakdown is displayed before submission. Verify that fees are calculated correctly per tier and zone.*

**FR-ORD-003: Order State Machine**
The system SHALL enforce the order state machine with transitions: Created → Awaiting Payment Verification → Queued for Assignment → Offered → Accepted → Travelling to Market → Shopping → Shopping Complete → Receipt Verified → In Delivery → Delivered → Completed → Archived. The system SHALL reject any transition that is not defined in the state machine. All transitions SHALL be logged with timestamp, event, and actor.
*Priority: Critical | BR: D-004, SM-01 through SM-10 | UC: All*
*AC: Verify that valid transitions succeed. Verify that invalid transitions (e.g., Offered → Delivered) are rejected.*

**FR-ORD-004: Item Status Tracking**
The system SHALL allow the shopper to mark each order item as: Found, Substituted, or Not Available. The system SHALL display real-time item status to the customer. The shopper MAY upload an optional photo when marking items as damaged, questionable, or suggesting a substitute.
*Priority: Critical | BR: D-006 | UC: UC-010*
*AC: Verify that item status changes are reflected in real time. Verify that optional photos are stored and displayed.*

**FR-ORD-005: Substitution Handling**
When an item is unavailable, the system SHALL apply the customer's preset substitution preference: (a) Best Match — shopper selects similar item guided by the customer's shopping preference, (b) Contact Me — shopper sends chat message with options and optional photo, system attempts customer contact (push + SMS), configurable timeout (default 3 min) with fallback policy (skip or Best Match), (c) No Substitutions — item skipped. The shopper MAY proactively suggest substitutions even when the original item is available but suboptimal.
*Priority: Critical | BR: D-007 | UC: UC-010*
*AC: Verify that Best Match does not require customer interaction. Verify that Contact Me sends notification and applies fallback on timeout. Verify that No Substitutions skips the item.*

**FR-ORD-006: Receipt Upload**
The system SHALL accept receipt uploads in multiple formats: (a) single itemised receipt photo, (b) multiple receipt photos, (c) handwritten receipt photo, (d) no-receipt manual price entry. All uploaded receipts SHALL be stored as evidence for billing and dispute resolution.
*Priority: Critical | BR: D-008 | UC: UC-011*
*AC: Verify that all four receipt types are accepted. Verify that manual entry triggers a quality spot-check flag.*

**FR-ORD-007: Final Price Calculation**
After receipt upload, the system SHALL calculate: Final Amount = Actual Item Cost + Tiered Service Fee (per F-001) + Delivery Fee (per F-002). If the final amount exceeds the estimate by MORE THAN 10% OR more than 10,000 TZS (whichever occurs first), the system SHALL request customer approval. If the customer does not respond within 5 minutes, the system SHALL process at the original estimate with any overshoot covered by the platform.
*Priority: Critical | BR: F-004, D-003 | UC: UC-011*
*AC: Verify that final calculation is correct. Verify that the 10% OR 10,000 TZS threshold triggers approval. Verify that 5-minute timeout applies the price guarantee.*

**FR-ORD-008: Shopping Timeout**
The system SHALL estimate shopping duration based on item count: ≤ 10 items — expected 30-45 minutes, 11-20 items — 45-60 minutes, > 20 items — 60-90 minutes. The system SHALL flag "Extended Shopping" when the shopper exceeds expected duration by 50%. Support SHALL be notified when duration exceeds double the estimate.
*Priority: High | BR: D-011 | UC: UC-010*
*AC: Verify that the correct time tier is applied based on item count. Verify that alert triggers at 50% over expected. Verify that support is notified at 100% over expected.*

**FR-ORD-009: Order Cancellation — Customer**
The system SHALL support customer cancellation with status-dependent fees: (a) Before acceptance (G-001) — no fee, pre-auth released, (b) After acceptance, before shopping (G-002) — compensation based on shopper's actual progress (distance + time, min 1,500 TZS, max 100% delivery fee), (c) After shopping started (G-003) — full delivery fee + 10% restocking fee, OR customer may choose to receive already-purchased items.
*Priority: High | BR: G-001, G-002, G-003 | UC: UC-005*
*AC: Verify that pre-acceptance cancellation has no fee. Verify that post-acceptance fee is calculated based on shopper progress. Verify that post-shopping cancellation offers the option to receive purchased items.*

**FR-ORD-010: Order Cancellation — Shopper**
The system SHALL allow a shopper to cancel an accepted order only for: market closed, verifiable emergency, customer unreachable, prohibited items, vehicle breakdown, safety threat, natural disaster, road closure, or system failure. Unauthorised cancellation SHALL result in escalating penalties.
*Priority: Critical | BR: G-004 | UC: UC-009*
*AC: Verify that valid reasons result in no penalty. Verify that invalid reasons apply penalty escalation.*

**FR-ORD-011: Scheduled Order Release**
The system SHALL release scheduled orders to the assignment engine based on estimated total lead time: travel time to market + estimated shopping duration + delivery travel time + configurable preparation buffer (recommended 15 minutes). If the system predicts insufficient shopper availability, the customer SHALL be notified at least 2 hours before the delivery window.
*Priority: High | BR: D-014 | UC: UC-002*
*AC: Verify that a scheduled order is released at the calculated time. Verify that supply shortage notification is sent at least 2 hours ahead.*

**FR-ORD-012: Order Archival**
The system SHALL archive completed orders 90 days after "Completed" status. Archived orders SHALL be retained for at least 1 year (or longer per legal/tax requirements). Orders with open disputes SHALL NOT be archived.
*Priority: Low | BR: D-013 | UC: N/A*
*AC: Verify that archival occurs at 90 days. Verify that disputed orders are not archived. Verify that archived data is retrievable for 1 year.*

#### 5.1.5 Delivery & Fulfillment

**FR-DEL-001: Delivery Radius**
Each zone SHALL define its own standard delivery boundary using road distance (C-005). Deliveries beyond the standard boundary MAY be offered only if: (a) a shopper explicitly accepts the extended trip, (b) the customer accepts the revised estimate and fee, (c) the additional fee is calculated using configurable zone pricing.
*Priority: High | BR: E-001, C-005, F-002 | UC: UC-012*
*AC: Verify that a delivery within zone boundary is auto-assigned. Verify that a delivery beyond boundary requires explicit shopper opt-in.*

**FR-DEL-002: Dynamic ETA Prediction**
Upon entering "In Delivery" status, the system SHALL calculate an Expected Delivery Time using: road distance, real-time traffic data, shopper location, and historical zone averages. The customer SHALL receive the predicted ETA. Alerts SHALL trigger when actual time exceeds prediction by > 30% OR > 15 minutes.
*Priority: High | BR: E-003 | UC: UC-012*
*AC: Verify that ETA is calculated and displayed to customer. Verify that alert triggers at correct threshold.*

**FR-DEL-003: ETA Recalculation**
During delivery, the system SHALL continuously monitor shopper progress. If the predicted arrival time changes by more than 5 minutes increase, the system SHALL: (a) recalculate ETA, (b) notify the customer of the updated ETA with reason, (c) notify the shopper if rerouting is recommended, (d) record the delay reason for analytics.
*Priority: High | BR: E-009 | UC: UC-012*
*AC: Verify that a 5+ minute ETA increase triggers recalculation. Verify that customer receives updated ETA with reason.*

**FR-DEL-004: Proof of Delivery**
The system SHALL require: (a) GPS-confirmed arrival within 100m of delivery address, (b) photo of delivered items, (c) customer confirmation in-app. The system SHALL support "Delivered to Authorized Recipient" with optional name, relationship, and photo for deliveries to family members, receptionists, security guards, or neighbours.
*Priority: Critical | BR: E-002 | UC: UC-012*
*AC: Verify that delivery requires GPS + photo + confirmation. Verify that Authorized Recipient option is available.*

**FR-DEL-005: Unavailable Customer**
If the customer is not available at delivery, the system SHALL: (a) send automatic notification to customer, (b) shopper calls via masked call, (c) shopper sends chat message, (d) after 5 minutes without response, if customer pre-authorised safe drop — leave items with photo; otherwise — return items to market.
*Priority: High | BR: E-004 | UC: UC-012*
*AC: Verify that 5-minute wait timer is enforced. Verify that safe drop requires pre-authorisation.*

**FR-DEL-006: Inspection Windows**
The system SHALL enforce category-based inspection windows: fresh produce/dairy — 5 minutes, fresh meat/fish — immediate (before shopper leaves), electronics/packaged goods — 24 hours, non-food general — 30 minutes. After the window, the order SHALL be auto-completed for those items.
*Priority: High | BR: E-007 | UC: UC-004*
*AC: Verify that each category has the correct window. Verify that auto-completion occurs after window expiry.*

**FR-DEL-007: Redelivery**
If delivery fails due to customer unavailability, the customer SHALL be able to request redelivery within 24 hours. The redelivery fee SHALL be: current delivery fee minus configurable discount (Repeat Customer Discount and/or Operational Discount). If the failed delivery was due to platform error, redelivery SHALL be free.
*Priority: Medium | BR: E-008 | UC: UC-004*
*AC: Verify that redelivery can be requested within 24 hours. Verify that platform error results in free redelivery.*

#### 5.1.6 Payments & Financial

**FR-PAY-001: Tiered Service Fee Calculation**
The system SHALL calculate the platform service fee on a tiered basis: 0 — 50,000 TZS: 8%, 50,001 — 150,000 TZS: 10%, 150,001 — 300,000 TZS: 12%, Above 300,000 TZS: 15%. Minimum fee: 1,000 TZS. The fee SHALL be calculated on the actual item cost from the receipt.
*Priority: Critical | BR: F-001 | UC: UC-002, UC-011*
*AC: Verify that each tier applies the correct percentage. Verify that a 30,000 TZS order charges 8% (2,400 TZS). Verify that a 200,000 TZS order charges 12% (24,000 TZS).*

**FR-PAY-002: Delivery Fee Calculation**
The system SHALL calculate the delivery fee using road distance with a configurable base fee and per-km rate per zone. The system SHALL use road distance (Maps API) not straight-line distance. Both base and per-km rates SHALL be configurable by administrators on a per-zone basis.
*Priority: Critical | BR: F-002 | UC: UC-002*
*AC: Verify that road distance is used. Verify that modifying zone pricing takes effect without code deployment.*

**FR-PAY-003: Pre-Authorisation Hold**
Upon order submission, the system SHALL place a pre-authorisation hold on the customer's mobile money account for the estimated total. If the hold fails, the system SHALL retry up to 2 times. If all retries fail, the order SHALL NOT be created.
*Priority: Critical | BR: F-003, F-011 | UC: UC-002*
*AC: Verify that a successful hold allows order creation. Verify that a failed hold after 3 attempts blocks order creation.*

**FR-PAY-004: Payment Capture**
Upon order delivery confirmation, the system SHALL charge the customer's mobile money account the final amount (actual item cost + service fee + delivery fee). If variance > 10% OR > 10,000 TZS, customer approval SHALL be required before capture.
*Priority: Critical | BR: F-004 | UC: UC-004, UC-012*
*AC: Verify that correct final amount is captured. Verify that variance threshold triggers approval workflow.*

**FR-PAY-005: Shopper Payout Calculation**
The system SHALL calculate the shopper's payout as: delivery fee (100%) + tiered shopping fee based on actual item cost (0-50,000: 2,500 TZS, 50,001-150,000: 4,000 TZS, 150,001-300,000: 6,000 TZS, Above 300,000: 2% max 15,000 TZS).
*Priority: Critical | BR: F-005 | UC: UC-013*
*AC: Verify that each tier applies the correct fee. Verify that a 30,000 TZS order pays 2,500 TZS shopping fee. Verify that a 500,000 TZS order pays 2% capped at 15,000 TZS.*

**FR-PAY-006: Shopper Settlement**
The system SHALL settle shopper earnings within 48 hours of order completion. Settlement SHALL transfer funds from "Pending Balance" to "Available Balance" in the shopper's wallet. High-performing shoppers (Platinum tier, V1) MAY qualify for 24-hour settlement.
*Priority: Critical | BR: F-006 | UC: UC-013*
*AC: Verify that settlement occurs at 48 hours. Verify that the shopper can withdraw available balance.*

**FR-PAY-007: First-Order Promotion**
The system SHALL apply a 50% discount on the delivery fee (capped at 2,000 TZS discount) for first-time customers on their first completed order.
*Priority: Medium | BR: F-007 | UC: UC-002*
*AC: Verify that a first-time customer receives the discount. Verify that returning customers do not receive it. Verify that the 2,000 TZS cap is enforced.*

**FR-PAY-008: Referral Programme**
The system SHALL credit the referring customer 5,000 TZS and the referred customer 2,500 TZS when the referred customer completes AND pays for their first successful order. Credits SHALL be applied to future service fees.
*Priority: Medium | BR: F-008 | UC: UC-001*
*AC: Verify that referral credit is only applied after the referred customer's first paid order. Verify that fraudulent self-referral is detected and penalised.*

**FR-PAY-009: Cash on Delivery**
The system SHALL offer Cash on Delivery as a payment option only to customers whose Customer Trust Score meets the minimum threshold (recommended: ≥ 60/100). COD orders SHALL be limited to a maximum of 100,000 TZS. The shopper SHALL receive a 2% cash handling fee on COD orders.
*Priority: High | BR: F-012 | UC: UC-002, UC-012*
*AC: Verify that a customer with a high Trust Score can select COD. Verify that a customer with a low Trust Score cannot. Verify that the 100,000 TZS limit is enforced.*

**FR-PAY-010: Shopper Protection (Stage-Based)**
The system SHALL compensate the shopper if an order cannot be completed due to customer action (payment failure, cancellation, unavailability) based on the stage reached: Stage 1 (traveling to market) — 1,500 TZS, Stage 2 (shopping started) — 4,000 TZS, Stage 3 (shopping complete, delivering) — full shopping fee + full delivery fee.
*Priority: Critical | BR: F-011 | UC: UC-005, UC-009*
*AC: Verify that Stage 1 compensation is 1,500 TZS. Verify that Stage 3 compensation is full fees. Verify that compensation is only paid when failure is not the shopper's fault.*

#### 5.1.7 Dispute Resolution

**FR-DSP-001: Dispute Lifecycle**
The system SHALL enforce a unified dispute lifecycle for all dispute types: (1) Issue Reported, (2) Evidence Collected, (3) Automated Validation (for straightforward cases), (4) Manual Review (assigned to support agent or ops admin), (5) Decision, (6) Execution, (7) Audit Logging.
*Priority: High | BR: G-011 | UC: UC-006, UC-015*
*AC: Verify that the dispute follows the defined lifecycle. Verify that each step is logged with timestamp and actor.*

**FR-DSP-002: Automated Triage**
The system SHALL automatically triage incoming disputes: (a) straightforward cases (clear evidence, < 10,000 TZS) — automated resolution per policy, (b) standard cases — assigned to support agent queue, (c) complex/high-value cases (> 100,000 TZS, behavioural) — escalated to Operations Admin. Any party MAY request escalation to human review.
*Priority: Medium | BR: L-008, G-011 | UC: UC-015*
*AC: Verify that a clear, low-value case is resolved automatically. Verify that a high-value case is escalated. Verify that escalation requests are honoured.*

**FR-DSP-003: Proportional Refund Tiers**
The system SHALL apply proportional compensation based on issue severity: (a) Minor — refund of affected item value only, (b) Moderate — refund of affected items + 10% of delivery fee, (c) Major (> 50% of order affected) — full refund OR partial refund of affected items + 30% delivery fee.
*Priority: High | BR: G-007 | UC: UC-006*
*AC: Verify that a minor issue refunds only the item value. Verify that a major issue offers the customer a choice. Verify that a single damaged tomato does not trigger a 30% delivery fee refund.*

**FR-DSP-004: Refund Processing**
The system SHALL process approved refunds through the customer's original payment method with a target of 24 hours from approval. Refunds SHALL be processed via mobile money (M-Pesa/Mixx). Disputed refunds MAY take longer pending resolution.
*Priority: High | BR: G-008 | UC: UC-006*
*AC: Verify that a refund is initiated within 24 hours of approval. Verify that the refund goes to the original payment method.*

**FR-DSP-005: Discretionary Goodwill**
The system SHALL support discretionary goodwill compensation: (a) minor inconvenience — refund only, no goodwill, (b) moderate inconvenience — refund + discretionary 5-10% goodwill, (c) significant failure — refund + goodwill up to 15% (capped at 10,000 TZS without Ops Admin approval).
*Priority: High | BR: G-009 | UC: UC-006*
*AC: Verify that goodwill is not automatically applied. Verify that amounts above 10,000 TZS require escalation.*

#### 5.1.8 Ratings & Quality

**FR-RATE-001: Customer Rating Submission**
The system SHALL prompt the customer to rate the shopper 1-5 stars with optional written feedback after order completion. Rating criteria: item accuracy, item quality, timeliness, communication, professionalism, overall experience. Ratings submitted more than 72 hours after completion SHALL NOT be counted.
*Priority: Critical | BR: H-001 | UC: UC-004*
*AC: Verify that the rating prompt appears after completion. Verify that ratings after 72 hours are accepted but not counted.*

**FR-RATE-002: Shopper Rating Submission**
The system SHALL prompt the shopper to rate the customer 1-5 stars after order completion. Criteria: communication clarity, responsiveness, availability, delivery information accuracy, payment cooperation, overall conduct. Customer ratings SHALL NOT be publicly displayed.
*Priority: High | BR: H-002 | UC: UC-013*
*AC: Verify that shopper rating prompt appears. Verify that customer ratings are not visible to other users.*

**FR-RATE-003: Recency-Weighted Rating Calculation**
The system SHALL calculate displayed ratings with greater weight given to recent ratings. The exact algorithm (exponentially weighted moving average or similar) is implementation-specific. Shoppers with fewer than 10 ratings SHALL display a simple average.
*Priority: High | BR: H-003 | UC: UC-004*
*AC: Verify that recent ratings influence the displayed rating more than old ones. Verify that shoppers with < 10 ratings show a simple average.*

**FR-RATE-004: Minimum Rating Threshold**
The system SHALL apply a minimum rating threshold of 4.0 (after 20+ completed ratings). Shoppers below 4.0 SHALL: (a) receive a quality warning, (b) have Assignment Score priority reduced, (c) if below 4.0 for 30 consecutive days, face progressive discipline per B-007.
*Priority: High | BR: H-004, B-007 | UC: UC-007*
*AC: Verify that a shopper with < 20 ratings is not judged against the threshold. Verify that a shopper below 4.0 for 30 days triggers discipline.*

**FR-RATE-005: Rating Fraud Detection**
The system SHALL detect and exclude ratings using multiple patterns: suspicious velocity (e.g., 20 five-star reviews in 5 minutes), reciprocal rating rings, device fingerprinting, IP address patterns, account age (new customer leaves 1-star), rating outliers, and coordinated manipulation.
*Priority: High | BR: H-005 | UC: UC-004*
*AC: Verify that a detected fraudulent rating is excluded from calculation. Verify that the customer who submitted the fraudulent rating is warned.*

**FR-RATE-006: Rating Blind Period**
The system SHALL hide ratings from both parties until: (a) both parties have submitted their rating, OR (b) 72 hours have passed since order completion — whichever comes first.
*Priority: Medium | BR: H-006 | UC: UC-004*
*AC: Verify that a rating submitted by one party is not visible to the other until both rate or 72 hours elapse.*

**FR-RATE-007: Risk-Based Quality Audits**
The system SHALL flag for quality audit: orders from new shoppers (first 20), orders with disputes/complaints, shoppers with unusual rating patterns, orders flagged by fraud detection, and orders with manual price entry. An additional random sample of normal orders SHALL also be audited (recommended: 1%, adjustable).
*Priority: Medium | BR: H-007 | UC: N/A*
*AC: Verify that flagged orders appear in the audit queue. Verify that random sampling is applied to normal orders.*

**FR-RATE-008: Reputation Recovery**
The system SHALL support reputation recovery through: (a) older negative ratings carrying less weight over time (H-003), (b) sustained high-quality performance improving standing, (c) coaching/retraining restoring performance status, (d) shoppers maintaining above-threshold performance for 60 consecutive days having prior warnings cleared.
*Priority: Medium | BR: H-008 | UC: UC-007*
*AC: Verify that a shopper who maintains good performance for 60 days has warnings cleared. Verify that recovery does not apply to fraud/theft/abuse.*

#### 5.1.11 Emergency & Exceptions

**FR-EMRG-001: Shopper Emergency Protocol**
If a shopper experiences an emergency (accident, illness, vehicle breakdown, personal emergency) while on an active order, the system SHALL allow them to trigger "Shopper Emergency" in the app. The system SHALL: (a) notify the customer that the shopper has an emergency, (b) notify platform support, (c) cancel the order with no penalty to the shopper, (d) release the pre-authorisation hold on the customer. The shopper MAY be required to provide a brief explanation within 30 minutes. Pattern abuse SHALL be investigated.
*Priority: Critical | BR: K-001 | UC: UC-009*
*AC: Verify that emergency trigger cancels the order with no penalty. Verify that customer and support are notified. Verify that pattern abuse is investigated.*

**FR-EMRG-002: Customer Emergency Handling**
If a customer has an emergency and cannot receive the delivery, support SHALL determine the appropriate action based on the circumstances. Options include: (a) hold the items for up to 4 hours, (b) deliver to an alternative contact designated by the customer, (c) cancel with partial refund per G-003.
*Priority: High | BR: K-002 | UC: UC-004*
*AC: Verify that support can apply each of the three options. Verify that cancel with refund works correctly.*

**FR-EMRG-003: Market Closure Procedure**
If a shopper arrives at a market and finds it closed, the system SHALL allow the shopper to mark "Market Closed". The system SHALL search for alternative markets within a configurable search radius (based on zone policy per C-007) of the original market. If an alternative is available, the shopper MAY be offered the option to shop there. If the alternative has significantly different pricing (expected variance > 15%), the customer SHALL be notified and must approve before shopping proceeds. If no alternative is available, the order SHALL be cancelled with no penalty.
*Priority: High | BR: K-003 | UC: UC-009*
*AC: Verify that alternative market search uses zone configurable radius. Verify that customer approves price variance > 15%.*

**FR-EMRG-004: Network Failure — Offline Continuation**
If a shopper loses network connectivity during an active order, the system SHALL: (a) continue operating in offline mode with cached data, (b) queue GPS points locally for transmission when connectivity resumes, (c) queue status changes locally, (d) allow critical actions (mark delivered) to be queued for batch sync. If connectivity is not restored within 30 minutes, the system SHALL attempt to reach the shopper via SMS. After 60 minutes, the order SHALL be escalated to support.
*Priority: High | BR: K-004 | UC: UC-010, UC-012*
*AC: Verify that offline mode caches order data and GPS. Verify that queued data syncs on reconnection. Verify that escalation occurs at 60 minutes.*

**FR-EMRG-005: Natural Disaster / Civil Unrest Protocol**
In the event of a natural disaster, civil unrest, or government-imposed curfew, the system SHALL allow an administrator to: (a) suspend operations in affected zones, (b) cancel all active orders in affected zones with full refunds, (c) release all pre-authorisation holds, (d) pay shoppers for time invested up to the point of suspension. Partial zone suspensions SHALL be supported.
*Priority: Critical | BR: K-005 | UC: UC-014*
*AC: Verify that a zone can be suspended independently. Verify that affected orders are cancelled with full refunds. Verify that shoppers are compensated.*

**FR-EMRG-006: Behavioural Complaint — Shopper**
If a customer reports inappropriate shopper behaviour (harassment, aggression, discrimination), the system SHALL: (a) immediately suspend the shopper pending investigation, (b) flag chat logs, rating history, and recordings for review, (c) enforce a 48-hour decision deadline, (d) if confirmed, permanently deactivate the shopper. If the complaint is found to be false or malicious, the customer SHALL receive a warning. Repeat false complainants SHALL be subject to suspension.
*Priority: Critical | BR: K-007 | UC: UC-006, UC-014*
*AC: Verify that reported shopper is immediately suspended. Verify that decision is made within 48 hours. Verify that false complaints result in customer warning.*

**FR-EMRG-007: Post-Inspection Missing Items**
If a customer reports an item missing after the inspection window has passed, the claim SHALL be generally denied. However, if the customer provides compelling evidence (video of unpacking, weight discrepancy), a discretionary review MAY be conducted. Decisions SHALL be evidence-based — no automatic special treatment for high-volume customers.
*Priority: Medium | BR: K-008 | UC: UC-006*
*AC: Verify that a claim without evidence is denied. Verify that a claim with compelling evidence can be escalated for review.*

#### 5.1.12 Administration & Operations

**FR-ADMIN-001: Tiered Access Control**
The admin dashboard SHALL support at least three access tiers: (a) Super Admin — full system access (maximum 2 people), (b) Operations Admin — shopper management, order management, dispute resolution, (c) Support Agent — dispute handling only with read-only access to order and user data. Temporary elevation of privileges SHALL require Super Admin approval and SHALL be logged.
*Priority: Critical | BR: L-001 | UC: UC-014*
*AC: Verify that each role has the correct permissions. Verify that privilege escalation is logged.*

**FR-ADMIN-002: Financial Audit Logging**
The system SHALL log every financial transaction (payment, refund, payout, fee charge, wallet adjustment) with: timestamp, amount, transaction type, user ID, order ID, payment reference, and admin ID (if manual). Logs SHALL be immutable (append-only). Corrections SHALL be made by adding a corrective entry, not modifying or deleting an existing one.
*Priority: Critical | BR: L-002 | UC: All*
*AC: Verify that financial transactions are logged. Verify that logs cannot be modified or deleted. Verify that corrective entries are appended.*

**FR-ADMIN-003: Regulatory Reporting**
The system SHALL generate the following reports for administrators: (a) monthly transaction summary by volume and value, (b) monthly shopper earnings report, (c) quarterly tax summary (for TRA compliance), (d) incident report (fraud cases, disputes, emergencies). Reports SHALL be exportable as CSV and PDF.
*Priority: High | BR: L-004 | UC: N/A*
*AC: Verify that each report type can be generated. Verify that export formats are available.*

**FR-ADMIN-004: SLA Monitoring**
The system SHALL monitor and report on key SLAs: order acceptance time (< 30 seconds target, dynamic), shopping completion time (tiered per D-011), delivery time (dynamic ETA per E-003), support response time (< 5 minutes for urgent, < 2 hours for standard), dispute resolution time (< 24 hours). SLA breaches SHALL trigger automated alerts. Persistent breaches (> 10% above target for 7 days) SHALL trigger a formal review.
*Priority: High | BR: L-005 | UC: All*
*AC: Verify that each SLA is monitored. Verify that breaches trigger alerts. Verify that persistent breaches trigger review.*

**FR-ADMIN-005: Platform Maintenance Window**
Scheduled maintenance SHALL occur between 03:00 and 05:00 EAT. Critical functionality (order processing, payment) SHALL NOT be taken offline during peak hours (08:00-21:00 EAT). Emergency maintenance outside the window SHALL require Super Admin approval. Users SHALL be notified of planned downtime at least 24 hours in advance.
*Priority: High | BR: L-006 | UC: N/A*
*AC: Verify that maintenance outside the window is blocked without approval. Verify that 24-hour notice is given.*

**FR-ADMIN-006: Terms of Service Acceptance**
All users SHALL accept the current Terms of Service and Privacy Policy at registration. Updated terms SHALL require re-acceptance at next login. Major changes (fees, data usage, liability, user rights) SHALL require express consent via OTP verification.
*Priority: Critical | BR: L-007 | UC: UC-001, UC-007*
*AC: Verify that registration is blocked without ToS acceptance. Verify that updated terms require re-acceptance. Verify that major changes trigger OTP consent.*

**FR-ADMIN-007: Business Continuity — Data Backup**
The system SHALL back up: (a) database — hourly incremental plus daily full backup, (b) media (photos) — daily backup. Backups SHALL be stored in a geographically separate location from primary infrastructure. Retention: daily backups for 30 days, weekly for 6 months, monthly for 2 years.
*Priority: Critical | BR: L-009 | UC: N/A*
*AC: Verify that backups run on schedule. Verify that backups are stored in a separate geographic location. Verify that retention policies are enforced.*

**FR-ADMIN-008: Live Platform Metrics Dashboard**
The admin dashboard SHALL display: (a) live orders map with ongoing order locations, (b) daily/weekly/monthly GMV, (c) active shopper count and utilisation rate, (d) order fulfillment rate, (e) average assignment time, (f) dispute rate, (g) new user registrations. Metrics SHALL refresh every 30 seconds for live data and hourly for aggregated data.
*Priority: High | BR: L-010 | UC: N/A*
*AC: Verify that each metric is displayed correctly. Verify that live data refreshes at 30-second intervals.*

#### 5.4.4 Integration Assumptions

The following assumptions govern the system's integration with external services and SHALL be validated before and during implementation:

| Assumption | Impact if False | Mitigation | Governing Rule/NFR |
|------------|----------------|------------|-------------------|
| M-Pesa and Mixx APIs provide at least 99.5% uptime and < 5 second response times | Payment capture and refunds degrade user experience | Multi-provider abstraction. Retry 2x (F-011). Fallback between providers. Offline queue for non-critical events. | NFR-AVL-007 |
| SMS gateway delivers OTP reliably across all Tanzanian mobile networks | Users unable to register or verify identity | Alternative SMS provider. Voice call OTP fallback. Queue/retry for 15 min before ops alert. | NFR-AVL-008 |
| Maps API provides accurate road distance data for urban Tanzanian roads | Delivery fee and ETA calculations are inaccurate | Fallback: Haversine × 1.5 empirical ratio. Historical travel time averaging to improve accuracy over time. | NFR-INT-006 |
| FCM delivers push notifications reliably to Android devices in Tanzania | Shoppers miss offers; SMS costs increase | SMS fallback within 5 seconds (J-003). Persistent local notification scheduling. | NFR-PERF-011 |
| Mobile money providers support pre-authorisation (hold) operations | Cannot prevent uncollectible orders | Direct capture as fallback. COD via Trust Score as alternative payment path. | F-003 |
| NIDA API may not be available at launch | Identity verification relies solely on document upload + manual review | PCC + manual review as primary path. NIDA integration as enhancement when available. | B-003 |
| Tanzania has no centralised address database | Delivery location relies on GPS + landmarks | Pin-drop as primary. Landmark and "near" descriptions. Evaluate what3words for V1. | E-005 |
| Dar es Salaam internet connectivity is sufficient for real-time services but may be intermittent | Temporary degradation during network drops | Offline queue (K-004). SMS fallback for critical notifications. 15-min cached order data. | NFR-AVL-009 |

#### 5.4.5 Implementation Constraints

| Constraint | Implication | Approach |
|------------|-------------|----------|
| **Mobile-First** — > 95% of users on mobile devices | All features designed for mobile first. Web admin is secondary. | Android MVP, iOS V1. Responsive patterns. Touch targets ≥ 48x48dp. |
| **Low-End Devices** — Many users have 1 GB RAM, Android 8.0+ devices | App must be lightweight and memory-efficient | APK ≤ 25 MB. Target API 26+. Test on Moto E5 reference device. Lazy resource loading. |
| **Data Cost Sensitivity** — Mobile data is expensive relative to income | Minimise data usage. No aggressive prefetching. | Data saver mode. Compressed images. ≤ 5 MB per 10-min session. CDN for static assets. |
| **Battery Life** — Shoppers use GPS for extended periods | Excessive polling drains batteries and causes churn | Tiered GPS per state (C-001). 5s pings only during active travel. Adaptive location updates. |
| **Payment Provider Limits** — M-Pesa may have daily transaction limits or batch windows | High-value orders or mass payouts may be delayed | Pre-auth at order time. 48-hour settlement batch. Cumulative transaction monitoring. |
| **Regulatory Uncertainty** — Gig economy and data protection laws evolving | Platform may need to adapt to future requirements | Flexible worker model (contractor → employee path). Privacy-by-design. Retention aligned with emerging standards. |
| **Offline Resilience** — Network coverage may be inconsistent in some delivery areas | Shopper app must function during intermittent connectivity | 15-min offline order cache. GPS queue + sync (K-004). Action queuing for status updates. |
| **Multi-Provider Payments** — Single provider dependency is a continuity risk | If M-Pesa has prolonged outage, platform revenue stops | Provider abstraction (F-002). Config-based switching. Both M-Pesa + Mixx at MVP. Independent provider health monitoring. |
| **No National Address System** — Addresses are descriptive, not structured | Delivery relies on customer-provided data of variable quality | GPS pin-drop mandatory. Landmark + description fields. GPS verification at delivery. |
| **PCC Processing Time** — Police Clearance Certificates may take weeks | Shoppers face delays before working | Provisional approval with 60-day deadline (B-003). Full clearance required for high-value orders. |
